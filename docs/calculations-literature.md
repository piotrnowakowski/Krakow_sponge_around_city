# Calculations and literature in the app

Open **About → Calculations & literature**, use the matching button in **Room for the river** or a ditch candidate's **Terrain & method**, or follow the link in **story step 5**. The section is available in English and Polish and uses the selected meander proposal's measured values (or the default example when none is selected). The ditch-ponding calculation has its own section, distinct from the channel-volume estimate.

## Calculation basis

- **Meanders:** current length `L₁`, proposed length `L₂`, extra length `L₂ − L₁`, percentage `100 × (L₂ − L₁) / L₁`. Distances use EPSG:2180. Amplitudes, target gain, screening cutoffs and obstacle buffers are project choices, not values validated by the literature. See [the reproducible method](meander-concepts.md).
- **Ditch estimate:** `V = mapped length × assumed cross-section × fill factor`, with the assumptions read from `retention.json`. The current values are 1.0 m² and 0.5, or 50 m³ for an illustrative 100 m ditch per filling. This is a channel-volume estimate, with no calculated recharge or peak-flow reduction.
- **Terrain ponding around a short filled ditch section:** use official GUGiK 1 m ground terrain and keep the remaining mapped drainage open. At each fill height, water level is the lower of the crest and the lowest escape saddle minus 0.01 m. Only connected below-level cells form the footprint. For 1 m² cells, `area = wet cell count × 1 m²` and `capacity = Σ max(0, water level − ground) × 1 m²`. Repeat without the patch and subtract natural depression storage in the same footprint to obtain added capacity. The map has six default proposals from fifteen coarse candidates. See [the complete method and reproducible checks](ditch-barriers-method.md).
- **Screening:** assume a 6 m across × 3 m along filled patch with no bed opening, and test heights 0.2–1.0 m. At the default 0.6 m, require at least 5 m³ added capacity, 10 m² outside the nominal ditch and 100 m clearance from the entire water footprint to buildings. Unknown baseline drainage and footprint escape into missing terrain or a tile edge exclude a default proposal. These are project choices, not validated safety thresholds.
- **Outflow:** open drainage can cap the pond before water reaches the fill crest. The optional crest-only example is `Q = C b H^(3/2)`, with `C = 1.5 m^(1/2)/s`, width `b` and head `H` in metres and `Q` in m³/s. It does not estimate bypass capacity, total network discharge, tailwater, seepage or event timing. Raising the illustrative overflow head does not change the contained-water footprint.
- **Interpretation:** terrain capacities need sufficient inflow. The model does not predict rainfall flooding, groundwater saturation or persistent ponds; 1 m horizontal resolution does not imply centimetre vertical accuracy. Candidate volumes cannot be added without modelling their interaction. The earlier Manning/notch channel-only experiment is retained separately in code and is not used for the blue ponding polygons.

## Reviewed sources and their role

The field studies provide evidence about flow redistribution and temporary storage; they do not calibrate Kraków's candidate sites. USACE terrain-storage guidance supports the geometric capacity method, while its weir reference supports only the illustrative crest discharge. This is an annotated methods bibliography, not a systematic literature review. Source pages and research abstracts were checked on 4 October 2026; the full Polish handbook was not reviewed in this update.
